6 questions to ask before greenlighting any agent project

Gartner predicts over 40% of agentic AI projects will be canceled by 2027 — not because the models fail, but because of escalating costs, unclear ROI, and missing risk controls. This is the six-item check I run before I'll say yes to a client's agentic AI project. Traffic-light scored so you know exactly where you stand before you write the first line of code.

6 QUESTIONS

  1. Decision Authority — Who can approve or override an action?
  2. Audit Trail — Can every action be traced to a decision point?
  3. Cost Ceiling — Is there a hard stop on spend or scope?
  4. Human Escalation Path — A defined trigger to hand back to a person?
  5. Data Access Scope — Least-privilege, not "give it everything"?
  6. Rollback Plan — Can it be turned off without breaking the workflow?
